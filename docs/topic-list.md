# SUSCOMP Topic List (course-provided)

Source: `project-ideas.pdf` (main list) + `project-ideas-seminars.pdf` (secondary list). We must pick one and narrow/modify it — not use it as-is.

## Main list (18)

| # | Title | SDGs | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hybrid Microgrid System | 7, 13 | Small wind + PV into a smart grid. Forecast production from weather, redirect surplus energy to charging a vehicle, computers, etc. |
| 2 | Drone-Based Thermal Energy Auditor | 11, 13 | Thermal-camera drones fly over campus/neighbourhood, find building heat-loss points, show results on smart glasses. |
| 3 | Examining tools for automated LCA | — | Install 3+ automated LCA tools, test on 10+ products against manufacturer-reported numbers, find over/under-reporting, propose methodology fixes. |
| 4 | Studying forced obsolescence of mobile phones | — | Analyse App/Play Store: app size growth, app obsolescence speed, OS update/support changes over time. Propose regulatory/technical fixes. |
| 5 | Mobile E-Waste Reuse Laboratory for Vulnerable Groups | 4, 10 | Disassemble old phones/computers, repurpose components into educational kits or assistive tech for disadvantaged communities. |
| 6 | Automatic Waste Sorter Using Computer Vision | 12 | Smart bin: camera + neural network recognises waste type, mechanical system sorts it. Optional points/competition app. |
| 7 | Haptic Navigator for the Blind in Smart Cities | 3, 10 | Haptic gloves + smart glasses/phone camera detect obstacles, signs, crossings; communicate via vibration. |
| 8 | Inclusive Device Control Using Micro-Gestures | 10 | Software lets people with physical disabilities control an RC car/drone/smart home via micro-gestures instead of a controller. |
| 9 | Eco-Drone for Monitoring Forest Health and Fires | 15 | Thermal-camera drones over forest/farmland; leaf temperature as drought indicator; early fire/smoulder detection. Variant: quadruped robot. |
| 10 | Smart Composter with Bio-Sensing | 12, 13 | E-waste-built sensors track temperature/humidity/gas during canteen compost decomposition; app notifies staff to optimise compost quality. |
| 11 | Autonomous Search System for Avalanche and Rubble Rescue | 3, 11 | Drones + thermal camera autonomously search disaster area for heat signatures, relay location to rescuers' smart glasses. |
| 12 | Haptic Simulator for Training Rescuers and Firefighters | 4, 8 | VR/AR simulator with smart glasses + haptic gloves simulates heat/hazard for training civil protection units. |
| 13 | System for Restoring Degraded Soil and Urban Farming | 2, 15 | PV-powered garden/greenhouse; thermal camera assesses plant hydration; app notifies community when/where to water. |
| 14 | Autonomous Herder and Biodiversity Guardian | 15 | RC car (PV-charged) + drone monitor wildlife/pastureland, recognise invasive species or track animals. |
| 15 | Sustainable Charging Station for the Homeless or Refugee Camps | 7, 10 | Small wind turbine + PV + reused phone batteries build a portable free charging station for vulnerable groups. |
| 16 | Digital Literacy Platform Built from E-Waste | 4, 5 | Revive old PCs with lightweight Linux, set up local server; run coding/digital-literacy workshops for disadvantaged groups. |
| 17 | Green Light: A Smart Assisted Pedestrian Crossing | 11, 10 | Camera/Bluetooth detects reduced-mobility or elderly pedestrians, auto-extends green light; audio cue for blind pedestrians via smart glasses. |
| 18 | Air Pollution Monitoring with Street-Level Drones | 3, 11 | PM2.5 sensor network (drones or fixed, solar-powered), real-time public map, alerts nearby schools/kindergartens. |

## Secondary list (7, software/measurement-oriented)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | SW code optimization for energy efficiency | Compare algorithms solving the same problem; measure CPU/RAM/energy (pyRAPL, Intel Power Gadget). Recommendations for sustainable programming. |
| S2 | Examples of HW lifecycle and e-waste | Pick one device; trace resource extraction → manufacturing → usage → disposal; estimate carbon footprint and reduction potential. |
| S3 | Green digitalization of the public sector | Analyse cases (e-uprava, e-health, distance ed); interviews/surveys; carbon footprint before/after digitalization. |
| S4 | The carbon cost of Entertainment Industry | Estimate carbon footprint of HD vs 4K streaming; compare platforms; LCA vs DVD/cinema/physical games. |
| S5 | Digital Hoarding and the Hidden Cost of Cloud Infrastructure | Estimate footprint of unread emails/cloud storage; psychology of hoarding; propose a decluttering framework. |
| S6 | Can Artificial Intelligence Be Green, or Is It Just Consuming Energy? | AI training energy vs idle server waste; carbon balance sheet; 2030 scenarios (adoption growth vs cleanup). |
| S7 | Programming Themes | Open-ended: smart-city sensor apps, AI-assisted trash sorting, gamification, apps using Slovenian open environmental data (podatki.gov.si). |

## Requirements that apply to every topic

- Must be **narrowed/modified by us** — not used as-is. Problem definition is our job.
- Every proposal must discuss **UNESCO SDGs** and the **rebound effect** (Introduction + Evaluation).
- Programming is optional; reading, research, critical thinking, and writing are mandatory.
